Write in light

Overwhite.

Your message is drawn at 1,189 nits on a plate at 999. Without headroom a screen renders both as the same white, so the plate comes out blank. With headroom the words are simply there. No key, no software, no decoder.

Read this first.

This hides nothing from anyone who has the screen for it.

Anyone who opens your file on better hardware than yours reads it immediately, and anyone who knows the technique recovers it in seconds with ordinary image tools. It is not encryption and it authenticates nothing.

Do not put anything here that would hurt you if it were read aloud.

How to read one

Open the PNG on a display with HDR headroom, in Safari 26+ or Chrome 136+. The words are just there. Nothing to install, nothing to decode. On any other screen it is a white rectangle, and the exposure slider is how you read one anyway.

What it is not

The whole format

  • Plate999 nits
  • Message1,189 nits
  • Contrast+18.9%
  • CarrierPNG · Rec.2100 PQ
  • Capacity90 chars

That is the entire specification, published on purpose. A technique whose security depends on nobody knowing it does not have any.
