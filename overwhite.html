Overwhite

bright screens can read.

Write something, and Overwhite prints it in light instead of ink. Anyone with a good screen just reads it. Everyone else gets a blank white card and no idea what they are missing.

Fun, not secret.

This is a party trick, not a lockbox. Anyone with a better screen than yours reads it instantly, and anyone who knows the trick can pull it out with ordinary image tools. Write accordingly.

The numbers

The card sits at 999 nits and your words at 1,189, a fixed 18.9% brighter. A screen without headroom renders both as the same white, so the words vanish into the card. A screen with headroom simply shows them.

How someone reads it

Send them the PNG. If they open it on a recent iPhone, a MacBook Pro, or any HDR monitor, the words are just there. Nothing to install and nothing to decode. On any other screen it is a white rectangle, which is half the fun.
